# Feedback questions for testers — Employment Support Version

Use these questions when sending the app to real users, employment advisers, work coaches, or potential self-employment/start-up support organisations.

## Core product questions
1. Did the workout plan make sense for your goal?
2. Did the plan feel realistic for your current fitness level?
3. Were the safety warnings clear?
4. Did the health/injury section ask for the right information?
5. What was confusing?
6. What is the first thing you would improve?
7. Would you use this again?
8. Would you pay for a full 4-week personalised plan?
9. What price feels fair: £4.99, £9.99, £14.99, £19.99, or subscription?

## Employment-support questions
1. Did the app ask enough about your work routine or daily commitments?
2. Did the plan fit around work, shifts, training, volunteering, or job-search activity?
3. Did the work activity question make sense, for example sedentary, standing, walking, lifting, repetitive movement, or mixed work?
4. Would a short pre-work, break-time, after-work, or rest-day option make the plan more useful?
5. Could this type of app support someone preparing to return to suitable work?
6. From an employment support point of view, what question is missing?

## Evidence and development questions
1. Does this project show digital skills development?
2. Does it show motivation to return to suitable work or explore self-employment?
3. Would it be suitable to include in an employment action plan or development pathway?
4. What would make the project look more credible to a potential employer, mentor, or business adviser?

## Do not ask only
- “Do you like it?”
- “Is the design nice?”

Those questions are weak. Ask whether it solves a real problem and whether it supports realistic work/life routines.
